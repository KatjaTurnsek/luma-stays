## Reflection on AI use

Using AI helped me learn how to ask more specific technical questions, compare possible solutions, and understand why some changes worked better than others. It was especially useful for explaining unfamiliar concepts, debugging deployment issues, preparing test data, and improving documentation. All AI suggestions were reviewed, tested, and adapted before being included. I used AI as a support tool to help me understand problems and compare solutions.
